## 0.1.3

Seeds for shalya 0.1.0: `approval-refused` on `replace_text(path, edits)`, plus `git-flow`, `notebook-edit`, `search-choice`, `memory-key`, `delegation`; rounds carry `rank`, and `warm_start` orders by it before model.

`fit_rounds` returns the rounds a warm start uses and every round it skipped with a reason (missing tool, signature mismatch, limit); `Finding.turn` says which turn of an `assess_history` corpus a finding came from; `install_round` copies a compiled round into the library and returns its path.
